Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about projects, pricing, payments, hosting, support and communication.

General

What services does KASITU Webs offer?
Website development, mobile applications, eCommerce stores, graphic design, business registration assistance, selected security/locator solutions and maintenance & support. Availability depends on the project.
How do I start a project?
Contact us with your requirements. We can discuss the project, clarify scope and provide a quotation where appropriate.
Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?
We may work with clients outside South Africa depending on the project, payment arrangements, communication and service requirements.

Pricing & payment

Are the prices on the website final?
Displayed prices are starting or package prices where indicated. Final pricing can depend on requirements, content, integrations, revisions, third-party services and scope.
Do I need to pay before work starts?
Payment and deposit requirements are stated in the applicable quotation or agreement. Work may only begin after the required payment or approval conditions have been met.
Can I cancel a project?
Cancellation is governed by the agreed terms and our Refund Policy. Third-party costs and work already completed may not be refundable.

Websites & support

Do you provide domains and hosting?
Domain and hosting arrangements can be discussed as part of a project. Third-party provider fees may be separate from development fees unless the quotation says otherwise.
Can you maintain my website after launch?
Yes. Maintenance and support can cover updates, fixes, security-related maintenance, content changes and other agreed support.
How long does a website take?
Timelines vary according to scope, content readiness, feedback, integrations and revisions. The agreed project timeline takes priority over general estimates.

Privacy

What happens to information I submit?
We use personal information for legitimate business purposes such as responding to enquiries, preparing quotations, providing services and communicating with clients. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
