Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on a device by a website. Similar technologies may also be used for preferences, analytics and security.

2. How KASITU Webs may use them

Cookies or local storage may be used for essential site functions, remembering preferences such as theme settings, security, analytics and understanding how visitors use the website.

3. Analytics

The KASITU Webs website may use Google Analytics or other analytics technologies to understand traffic and improve the site. Analytics providers may collect technical and usage information according to their own policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality. Where a consent mechanism is required for a particular cookie or processing activity, it should be handled according to applicable law and the site's consent controls.

5. Third-party content

External services such as fonts, icons, payment tools, embedded content or analytics may use their own technologies. Their policies apply to their processing.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its technology changes.
