Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Please read before paying. Project-specific quotations or agreements may contain more specific cancellation, deposit or refund terms. Applicable consumer-protection law takes precedence where it applies.

1. Deposits and work already performed

Deposits or upfront payments may cover planning, design, development time, reservations, setup and other work. Where work has already been performed, the amount attributable to completed or committed work may not be refundable, subject to applicable law.

2. Client cancellation

A client may request cancellation by contacting KASITU Webs in writing. We will assess the stage of the project, work completed, outstanding obligations and third-party costs before determining any amount due or refundable.

3. Completed digital work

Where a digital service or deliverable has been completed and accepted, a refund will generally not be available solely because the client later changes their mind, except where required by applicable law or the project agreement.

4. Third-party costs

Domain registrations, hosting, premium software, licences, payment-provider charges, advertising spend and other third-party fees may be non-refundable because they are charged by external providers.

5. Revisions and scope changes

Requests that fall outside the agreed scope are not treated as defects or grounds for a refund. They may be quoted as additional work.

6. Defects and corrections

If an agreed deliverable contains a material defect attributable to KASITU Webs, the first remedy will normally be reasonable correction or completion in accordance with the agreed scope.

7. Delays or client non-cooperation

Where a project is delayed because required content, approvals, access or payments are not supplied, this does not automatically create a refund entitlement. We may pause or reschedule the project.

8. Refund method and timing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be returned using a reasonable available payment method. Processing times can depend on the payment provider or banking system.

9. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable South African consumer-protection legislation.

10. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email info@kasituwebs.co.za with your name, project/service, payment date, reason for the request and relevant reference or proof of payment. We may request additional information before making a decision.
