Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Privacy matters. KASITU Webs aims to handle personal information responsibly and transparently. This policy should be reviewed and updated as the site's forms, analytics, providers and business processes change.

1. Who we are

KASITU Webs is the business responsible for the processing described in this notice. Contact: info@kasituwebs.co.za; phone / WhatsApp Business 079 348 0103.

2. Information we may collect

  • Name and contact details.
  • Business and project information you choose to provide.
  • Messages, quotation details and support communications.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, approximate location and pages visited where collected by analytics or server systems.
  • Payment or transaction information handled through applicable payment providers. We do not intend to store full card details ourselves.

3. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, provide services, manage projects, communicate with clients, provide support, process transactions, maintain security, improve the website and meet legal or contractual obligations.

4. Legal basis and South African privacy requirements

Where applicable, processing is carried out on a lawful basis such as consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation or legitimate business purposes, taking into account the requirements of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

5. Sharing information

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the business or deliver a service, such as hosting, email, payment, analytics, communications or software providers. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

6. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, preferences, security or analytics. See our Cookie Policy for more information.

7. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, contractual or business records, security, dispute handling or legal requirements.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary contact forms or chat.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and reasonable verification, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, objection to or deletion of personal information, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also contact the relevant South African Information Regulator where appropriate.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and general users and are not deliberately directed at children. Please do not submit a child's personal information unless you are authorised to do so.

11. External websites

Our website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or requests, email info@kasituwebs.co.za. A privacy request may require identity verification to protect personal information.
